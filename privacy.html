Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Who uses this app

yta-channel is a private tool operated by one person, the owner of a single YouTube channel. Only that owner signs in. No one else's Google account is accessed.

Google data the app accesses

  • YouTube upload (youtube.upload): to upload and schedule the owner's own videos and thumbnails.
  • YouTube read-only (youtube.readonly): to check the publishing status of the owner's own videos.
  • YouTube Analytics read-only (yt-analytics.readonly): to read view counts and average view percentage for the owner's own videos.

How the data is used

Data is used only to publish the owner's videos and to decide which future video topics perform well. It is not sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Storage

The sign-in token and a small history of the channel's own video statistics are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is stored on any server operated by this app.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which stops the app from accessing the account.

Google API Services User Data Policy

yta-channel's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
